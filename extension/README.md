# 🧩 Insight.ai - Chrome Extension (Manifest V3)

YouTube AI Co-Pilot & Tutor that watches YouTube videos with you in a dedicated Chrome Side Panel, answering questions, creating quizzes, and jumping to exact timestamps in real-time.

---

## 🚀 How to Install in Google Chrome (Developer Mode)

### Step 1: Open Chrome Extensions Manager
1. In Google Chrome, navigate to `chrome://extensions` in your address bar.
2. In the top right corner, toggle on **Developer mode**.

### Step 2: Load the Extension
1. Click the **Load unpacked** button (top left).
2. Select this `extension` folder.
3. You will see **Insight.ai - YouTube AI Co-Pilot & Tutor** appear in your list of extensions!

### Step 3: Pin and Use on YouTube
1. Click the puzzle icon in Chrome's top right toolbar and pin 📌 **Insight.ai**.
2. Open any video on YouTube (e.g. `https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=...`).
3. Click the Insight.ai icon or press your Side Panel shortcut.
4. The Side Panel opens docked right next to the YouTube video!

---

## ⚡ Features
- **Auto-Detects YouTube Videos**: Automatically syncs with whichever video you are currently watching.
- **Clickable Timestamp Seek**: Clicking any timestamp (e.g., `[04:20]`) automatically seeks the active YouTube tab player to that exact second!
- **Detail Level Toggle**: Choose between **⚡ Short** (concise summary with timestamps) and **📚 Detailed** (in-depth conceptual breakdown).
- **Interactive Quizzes**: Generate multiple-choice questions to test your comprehension.
- **Flexible Backend**: Connects to the local Insight.ai web server or operates standalone with a direct Gemini API key.
